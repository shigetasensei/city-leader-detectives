🗾 Who Is Who in Hyogo?

Japan is split into smaller and smaller places. Each place has its own government. Read from top to bottom:

🇯🇵 Japan 日本

The whole country. The national government is in Tokyo.

⬇️ Japan is divided into prefectures

🗾 Prefecture 県 (ken): Hyogo

A big area made of many cities and towns. The leader is the Governor (知事). Hyogo's capital is Kobe. Hyogo has 41 cities and towns in all.

⬇️ Each prefecture is divided into cities and towns

🏙️ City 市 (shi)

Bigger and busier. Hyogo has 29 cities. The leader is the Mayor (市長).

Examples: Kobe, Nishinomiya, Ashiya

🏘️ Town 町 (machi / chō)

Smaller, with more countryside. Hyogo has 12 towns. The leader is the Town Mayor (町長).

Examples: Sayo, Kamigori, Shin'onsen

Cities and towns are the same level of government. Hyogo has no villages (村).

⬇️ Only the very biggest cities are divided again

📍 Ward 区 (ku)

A ward is a smaller part inside a very big city. Kobe has 9 wards, like Higashinada Ward. Each ward has a ward office and a Ward Mayor (区長). Nishinomiya and Ashiya do not have wards.

🔎 Our three places

  • Kobe = a city (and Hyogo's capital) with 9 wards. Higashinada is one ward.
  • Nishinomiya = a city, no wards.
  • Ashiya = a city, no wards.

So when you hear "Hyogo", think big (prefecture). When you hear "Kobe", think city. When you hear "Higashinada", think ward.
