🔍 Detective Rule: Check the Source

Tags on this page:

  • Official website made by a government or the mayor's office.
  • Newspaper written by reporters. Check the date of the article.
  • Other website made by a company, a fan, or a school. Check it against an official source.

Before you believe a fact, ask three questions:

  1. Who made it? A city office, a newspaper, a company, or a person? Official pages from the government are usually the most reliable for facts about the city.
  2. Who is it for? Is it written for residents, tourists, voters, or kids? Advertising and fan pages can leave things out.
  3. When was it made? Facts change. Check the date, and check newer sources if the page is old.

Was AI used? A page may not say. Look for a note, and if there is none, ask. 🎮 Try the Person or AI? game

If you cannot answer all three questions, do not trust the fact yet. Ask a teacher or look for a second source.
